Navigation & commands, the vcad way

Five pieces, one rule: every surface is reachable from the keyboard, shows the shortcut it teaches, and runs the same commands as the menu bar.

Halo, the radial

  • Right-drag anywhere in the viewport. The ring opens under the pointer; flick toward a verb and release. Plain right-click still opens the context menu.
  • Eight slots, laid out by direction so muscle memory holds. North is always Find, south is always Frame. The other six follow what is selected: a face gives Sketch on Face, Extrude, Section Here; a part gives Hide, Isolate, Combine; nothing selected gives Sketch, Add Box, Views.
  • ⌥⌘. opens it at the pointer, 1–8 or arrows pick, Esc closes.

Find, the command search

  • ⇧⌘P. Fuzzy match over every menu command, tool, view and saved view. Word-start hits rank first; recents float to the top when the query is empty and break ties after.
  • ↑↓ to move, Return to run. Each row shows its group and shortcut, so the palette teaches the menu.

Compass, the orientation widget

  • A live cube in the corner that turns with the camera. 26 targets: click a face, edge or corner and the camera eases there along the shortest arc.
  • Click the face you are already looking at and it flips to the opposite side. The arrows roll the view 90°, and the house resets to iso.

Saved views

  • ⌥⌘V saves the current camera under a name, remembered per document. Saved views show up in Camera ▸ Views, in Find, and in the Halo.

Section in one gesture

  • ⌥⌘X cuts through the selection, or the model's centre, on the axis that faces you most. With a face selected, the cut sits on that face's plane. Press again to close it.
