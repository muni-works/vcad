A real CAD app, not a demo.

modeling

model directly

Fusion-style selection and orbit. Press-pull with live dimensions. Sketches with inference, smart dimensions and trim/extend. History you can reorder, suppress and roll back.

drawings

one click to a sheet

Front, top and section views with dimensions, a BOM and a title block. Export to PDF or DXF.

fabrication

orient, slice, print

Send a part to your printer without leaving vcad. No slicer hand-off.

assemblies

huge assemblies, fast

Mates, interference checks and exploded views. Import and export STEP. A Voron 2.4 opens in 22 seconds.

Also: autosave and version history, antialiased edges and STEP colours. experimental image-to-CAD from photos.

Your agent works in your window.

  • Bring your own agent. Claude Code, Cursor, or anything that speaks MCP connects to vcad.app on your machine. There's no hosted server.
  • Watch it work. The agent's cursor moves in your viewport, its edits land live, and long jobs like FEA show in the header pill, where you can cancel them.
  • Every claim has a receipt. Clearances, masses and pitches carry a badge that reads Holds, Stale or Violated, and it re-checks on every edit.
