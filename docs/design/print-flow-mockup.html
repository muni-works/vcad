Print on P1S-shop?

The sliced job uses the AMS slots shown in the panel. The bed looks clear on the camera. vcad uploads over your local network and starts the job.
Elegance pass: no boxed cards or orange warning list. Sections are separated by space, with a hairline only above the footer. The stepper is four thin progress segments. Each step has one blue button, always in the same place at the bottom. Export is a quiet line of text links under it, on every step. Printability is a single sentence with a dot and a fix link, and the faces are tinted on the part. Slice settings come down to a material, a quality control, and an "Advanced" disclosure that holds today's full controls.
