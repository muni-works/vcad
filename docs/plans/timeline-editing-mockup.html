Timeline editing in vcad

Drag the blue marker to roll back. Click a feature to edit it, drag a chip to reorder, or try the scenarios. Each chip shows its rebuild state, eval time and cache state. The viewport shows features past the marker as ghosts, so you can see what an edit will do before you commit it.

Live downstream preview

While you drag a sketch dimension, every downstream feature keeps re-resolving in the background. A fillet turns amber or red as you drag, before you commit, not after.

Legal drops only

Reordering shows green gaps where the feature can go and red where it would come before its own inputs. The tooltip names the feature it depends on.

Errors that say what to do

A broken reference names the lost edge, explains in geometry terms why it is gone, and offers a fix: re-pick from ranked candidates, accept a split, suppress, or undo the edit that caused it.

Suppress is not an error

Features that depend on a suppressed feature are shown grey as skipped, with the reason. They come back unchanged when you unsuppress.
